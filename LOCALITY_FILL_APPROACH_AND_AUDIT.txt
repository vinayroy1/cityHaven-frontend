1. THE FILLING APPROACH & ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Problem:
Generic datasets contain administrative post office labels (e.g. "DLF QE", "Sohna Adda") 
and omit standard Indian real estate naming patterns such as:
- Numbered sectors (e.g., "Sector 57", "Sector 62", "Sector 14")
- Phased developments (e.g., "DLF Phase 1-5", "Sushant Lok 1-3", "Alpha/Beta/Gamma")
- Micro-market corridors (e.g., "Golf Course Road", "Dwarka Expressway", "Noida Expressway")

Our Solution:
1. Pincode Mapping:
   Every locality is assigned its authentic 6-digit postal PIN code from India Post.
2. Standardized Naming:
   Names match user search patterns exactly: "Sector <N>", "Phase <N>", "<Locality> Extension".
3. Strict City Scoping:
   Each locality has a `cityId` and `cityName` matching `india-cities-with-codes.json`.
4. Deduplication & Non-Destructive Merging:
   Entries are keyed by `cityName::name` so existing entries remain intact while gaps are filled.
5. In-Memory Inverted Index:
   Prefix & token inverted indices are rebuilt upon backend startup in ~1.2 seconds 
   for 40,000+ locations, returning sub-millisecond search latencies.
