PART 2: PROPERTY TYPES, SPECS & RENT/PRICE BENCHMARKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[A] RESIDENTIAL FOR RENT
- 1 RK / Studio Apartment:
  Carpet Area: 300 - 500 sq.ft | Rent: Rs 12,000 - 25,000/mo
  Ideal for: Solo professionals, bachelors, students.
- 2 BHK Family Apartment:
  Carpet Area: 900 - 1,250 sq.ft | Rent: Rs 22,000 - 45,000/mo
  Ideal for: Nuclear families, young couples, 2 tech flatmates.
- 3 BHK Luxury Apartment:
  Carpet Area: 1,400 - 2,200 sq.ft | Rent: Rs 35,000 - 85,000/mo
  Features: Gated society, clubhouse, power backup, parking.
- Independent Builder Floor / House:
  Carpet Area: 1,100 - 2,500 sq.ft | Rent: Rs 25,000 - 65,000/mo

[B] RESIDENTIAL FOR BUY / SELL
- 2 BHK / 3 BHK Ready-to-Move Flats:
  Area: 1,000 - 1,800 sq.ft | Price: Rs 55 Lakh - 1.8 Crore
  Features: RERA registered, OC received, bank loan ready.
- Luxury Floors / Villas / Kothi:
  Area: 2,000 - 4,500 sq.ft | Price: Rs 1.8 Crore - 6+ Crore

[C] PG / CO-LIVING / HOSTELS
- Private Single Room (with attached washroom):
  Rent: Rs 12,000 - 24,000/mo (Includes AC, 3 meals, WiFi, housekeeping).
- Double / Triple Sharing Room:
  Rent: Rs 6,500 - 12,000/bed/mo (Mess food, security, laundry).
- Tags: "Boys PG", "Girls PG", "Unisex Co-living".

[D] FLATMATE VACANCIES
- Single room available in occupied 2 BHK / 3 BHK:
  Rent: Rs 10,000 - 22,000/mo + shared utility expenses.

[E] COMMERCIAL PROPERTIES
- Ready-to-Move Office Space (Furnished):
  Area: 1,000 - 4,000 sq.ft (15-50 seats, cabins, conference, pantry).
  Rent: Rs 60 - 130 / sq.ft / mo.
- Bare-shell Corporate Floor:
  Area: 3,000 - 15,000 sq.ft | Long lease corporate setup.
- Retail Commercial Shop / Showroom:
  Area: 250 - 1,500 sq.ft | Ground floor / high footfall high-street.
  Rent: Rs 35,000 - 2,50,000/mo depending on location.

[F] PLOTS & LAND
- Residential Gated Plots:
  Size: 100 - 300 sq.yards (900 - 2,700 sq.ft) | Freehold title.
- Commercial / SCO / Highway Plots:
  Size: 200 - 1,000 sq.yards | Suited for shops or warehouse.
