## 2. All Property Categories & Sub-Types to Source

For each target locality, source 1–2 listings per sub-type to ensure every search filter on Awasio returns real results:

### Category A: Residential RENT (`/rent`)
1. **1 RK / Studio Apartment**:
   - *Target Audience*: Single bachelors, young software engineers, students.
   - *Typical Specs*: 300–500 sq.ft, semi or fully furnished, with lift & bike parking.
   - *Target Rent*: ₹12,000 – ₹25,000/mo (depending on city).
2. **2 BHK Family / Executive Flat**:
   - *Target Audience*: Nuclear families, couples, working professionals.
   - *Typical Specs*: 950–1,300 sq.ft, 2 bathrooms, 1 balcony, car parking, gated security.
   - *Target Rent*: ₹22,000 – ₹45,000/mo (metro average).
3. **3 BHK Luxury Apartment**:
   - *Target Audience*: Established families, senior executives.
   - *Typical Specs*: 1,400–2,200 sq.ft, 3 bathrooms, modular kitchen, full society amenities (clubhouse, pool, power backup).
   - *Target Rent*: ₹35,000 – ₹85,000/mo.
4. **Independent Builder Floor / Villa**:
   - *Target Audience*: Tenants wanting independent living without high-rise constraints.
   - *Typical Specs*: Ground or 1st/2nd floor with private parking and terrace access.

---

### Category B: Residential BUY / SELL (`/buy`)
1. **Ready-to-Move 2 BHK & 3 BHK Apartments**:
   - *Target Audience*: Homebuyers seeking immediate possession.
   - *Typical Specs*: Carpet area 750–1,250 sq.ft, RERA approved, OC received, 80%+ bank loanable.
   - *Price Band*: ₹55 Lakh – ₹1.8 Crore (varies by city).
2. **Luxury Floors / Kothi / Independent Villas**:
   - *Target Audience*: Premium HNIs, businessmen.
   - *Price Band*: ₹1.8 Crore – ₹6+ Crore.

---

### Category C: PG / Co-Living (`/pg`)
1. **Private Single Room**:
   - *Features*: AC, attached washroom, high-speed WiFi, 3-time meals included, daily housekeeping, washing machine access.
   - *Price*: ₹12,000 – ₹24,000/mo.
2. **Double / Triple Sharing Room**:
   - *Features*: Budget-friendly bed space, wardrobe, common food mess, CCTV security.
   - *Price*: ₹6,500 – ₹12,000/mo per bed.
3. **Gender Specifics**: Ensure a mix of **"Girls PG"**, **"Boys PG"**, and **"Unisex Co-living"**.

---

### Category D: Flatmates (`/flatmate`)
1. **Room Available in Pre-occupied 2/3 BHK**:
   - *Listing Pitch*: *"Master bedroom with attached bath available in fully furnished 3BHK high-rise gated society. Looking for easygoing male/female flatmate."*
   - *Price*: ₹10,000 – ₹22,000/mo + shared bills (cook, maid, WiFi).

---

### Category E: Commercial Properties (`/commercial`)
1. **Ready-to-Move Office Space**:
   - *Specs*: 1,000 – 4,000 sq.ft, plug-and-play with 15–50 workstations, conference room, manager cabins, pantry, 100% DG power backup.
   - *Rental*: ₹60 – ₹120 per sq.ft.
2. **Bare-Shell Corporate Space**:
   - *Specs*: Unfurnished office floor for long lease corporate setups (3–9 year lock-in).
3. **Commercial Retail Shop / Showroom**:
   - *Specs*: Ground floor or high street corner shop (250–1,500 sq.ft) with heavy footfall.
   - *Use Cases*: Grocery/Mart, Salon, Pharmacy, Cafe/Boutique.

---

### Category F: Plots & Land (`/plot`)
1. **Residential Gated Plots**:
   - *Specs*: 100 – 300 sq.yards (900 – 2,700 sq.ft), DTCP/BMRDA/GMDA approved, freehold title, boundary wall with road connectivity.
2. **Commercial / Mixed-Use Plots**:
   - *Specs*: Highway or main sector road facing plots suited for SCO (Shop-cum-Office) or warehousing.

## 4. Standard Listing Template for `awasio.com/propertyListing`

When creating a property on `https://awasio.com/propertyListing`, use this uniform structure for maximum SEO visibility and user trust:

### Template Example (2 BHK Rent in HSR Layout):
- **Listing Type**: `RENT`
- **Property Type**: `Residential` -> `Flat / Apartment`
- **Title**: `Spacious Semi-Furnished 2 BHK Flat for Rent in HSR Layout Sector 2, Bengaluru`
- **Society / Project Name**: `Greenwood Residency` (or local apartment name)
- **City**: `Bengaluru` | **Locality**: `HSR Layout` | **Sub-locality**: `Sector 2`
- **Price**: `₹32,000` | **Deposit**: `₹1,50,000` | **Maintenance**: `₹3,000`
- **Configuration**: `2 Bedrooms`, `2 Bathrooms`, `1 Balcony`
- **Super Built-Up Area**: `1,150 sq.ft` | **Carpet Area**: `950 sq.ft`
- **Furnishing**: `SEMI_FURNISHED` (Wardrobes, Modular Kitchen, Chimney, Fans, Lights)
- **Floor**: `3rd of 5 Floors` | **Facing**: `EAST`
- **Key Features & Description**:
  ```text
  Bright and naturally ventilated 2 BHK apartment located in prime HSR Layout Sector 2. 
  Just 5 minutes from 27th Main commercial street and 10 minutes to Silk Board Metro.
  
  Highlights:
  - Gated community with 24x7 security & CCTV
  - 100% DG power backup & Kaveri water supply
  - Covered reserved 4-wheeler and 2-wheeler parking
  - Ideal for families or working tech professionals
  - Ready for immediate move-in. Zero brokerage direct listing.
  ```
- **Photos**: 3–5 clean real photos (Living room, bedroom, modular kitchen, bathroom, building exterior).
